Precision polymer engineering

Polymers without the average.

We build polymers one molecule at a time, then use that precision to control structure: at surfaces, on nanoparticles, and in imaging agents.

Cain Department of Chemical Engineering
Louisiana State University

Approach

A conventional polymer sample is a mixture of many different molecules. Whatever you measure is an average over that mixture.

So we make samples in which every molecule is the same. Change one feature of the structure, and we can measure what that feature alone does.

Uniform side chains let bottlebrushes align. Same chemistry, two side-chain distributions. Disperse brushes pack without order, and discrete brushes align. Coarse-grained molecular dynamics at the air–water interface.
