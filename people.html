Principal Investigator

Jimmy Lawrence

Associate Professor, Cain Department of Chemical Engineering,
Louisiana State University

Jimmy Lawrence trained in chemical engineering at the University of Tokyo, took his PhD in Polymer Science and Engineering at the University of Massachusetts Amherst with Todd Emrick, and was a postdoctoral researcher with Craig Hawker at the University of California, Santa Barbara. Between those degrees he spent four years at Schlumberger, first as a research engineer in Japan and then as a research scientist at Schlumberger-Doll Research in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

He joined LSU in 2018. The group he runs builds polymers with molecularly exact architecture and uses them to ask what structure alone controls: how side-chain stiffness sets the glass transition, how brush architecture governs fluorine-19 MRI performance, and how sequence and flexibility decide what a chain does at an interface.

The work is supported by an NSF CAREER award and an NIH Maximizing Investigators' Research Award.
