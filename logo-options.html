Logo concepts

Three directions for comparison. Nothing here is live. The site currently uses the plain wordmark, now set larger in the header.

Concepts

1 · Collapse

A broad distribution of chain lengths resolving into a single line. This is the literal content of "polymers without the average", and it is the same idea as the figure on the home page. Reads at small sizes because the contrast is shape, not detail.

2 · Brush

The molecule itself: a backbone with evenly spaced side chains. The most literal mark, and the most immediately legible to a polymer chemist. The risk is that it describes one project rather than the group.

3 · Single peak

A chromatography trace narrowing to one species, with the broad trace left faint behind it. The most abstract of the three and the one that survives being shrunk to a favicon. It also says "we measure this", which the other two do not.

All three are SVG, single colour on ink #1D1D1F with the secondary mark in graphite #6E6E73. No gradients, no shadows, no raster. Each scales to a favicon or a poster without change. Say which direction to develop and it becomes the header mark.
