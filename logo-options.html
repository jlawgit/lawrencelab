Logo concepts

Five directions in the site's own language: beads, ink, and blue for discrete. Each is shown on light and dark, as a lockup with the name, and at favicon size.

Concepts

A · Discrete brushRecommended

One discrete bottlebrush: a backbone with five identical side chains in blue. It is the DBP from the home page reduced to a symbol, so the logo and the site tell the same story. Reads at favicon size.

32 and 16 px

B · Đ

The dispersity symbol on an ink tile, with one blue bead for a single species. Every polymer chemist reads it at once, and it makes the strongest favicon and social avatar of the set.

32 and 16 px

C · L monogram

A bottlebrush bent into an L, with identical side chains on the outside. The only mark that carries the name. Busier than A at small sizes.

32 and 16 px

D · Disperse to discrete

Uneven grey columns of beads become equal blue columns, read left to right. The argument of the lab in one line. Best as a wide mark, weaker as a favicon.

32 and 16 px

E · Single peak

A broad distribution with one resolved species in blue. Speaks to the separation work. Simple and quiet.

32 and 16 px

All marks are SVG in ink #1D1D1F, graphite #6E6E73 and blue #0071E3, with no gradients or shadows. B pairs well with A: A as the logo, B as the favicon and social avatar. Once a direction is chosen, the lettering gets converted to outlines for the final files.
