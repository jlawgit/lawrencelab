Logo concepts

Precision, functional material and building blocks, in a form meant to last thirty years. The marks we explored on the way are kept below for comparison.

Timeless

Built only from squares and a circle on a strict grid: one gap (a sixth of a unit) and one corner radius (a seventh of a unit) everywhere, with the circle drawn 3.5% larger so it reads the same size as a square. Each mark is tested in colour, in one colour, on dark, and at 16 px.

SeatedRecommended

One solid L-shaped block with a circle seated exactly in its corner, the same gap from both faces. A precisely shaped building block holding a functional unit, the way a ligand holds a particle. Reads as "L." at a glance. Unlike the two grids below, it does not look like an interface icon.

Three and one

Three identical squares and one circle on a 2 by 2 grid. The squares are building blocks, the circle is function (a particle, an active unit), and the circle fits its cell exactly, which is precision. The three squares also form an L. Simple enough to draw from memory, but close to Google's Material widgets icon.

Lattice

Eight identical squares around one circle on a 3 by 3 grid. A material built from exact units, with function placed precisely at its centre. Reads more as a material than as a letter. Risks looking like an app grid or keypad.

Explored: J and L

JL · Tetris pieces

A J and an L, back to back, as true Tetris pieces. Reads as JL and as two blocks that fit. At this height it looks more like an upturned T than a peak.

32 and 16 px

JL · Tall

One block taller, so the stems rise like a single sharp peak above a flat baseline: a monodisperse MALDI spectrum. The two tones keep J and L readable. The last tile shows LSU purple and gold.

32 and 16 px

JL · One colour

The same shape in one colour. The seam becomes the centre line of the peak, so the spectrum reading comes first and the letters second.

32 and 16 px

JL · Blocks

Every block visible. Tetris and a bar spectrum at once, the most literal about building blocks. Busier at 16 px.

32 and 16 px
Explored: LL

1 · LL lock

Two identical L blocks lock into a 2 by 4 rectangle with nothing left over. It reads as LL, as building blocks, and as a perfect fit, and it keeps the tall column of the 2018 logo. The last tile shows it in LSU purple and gold.

32 and 16 px

2 · One block

A single L made of four identical units. The simplest form of the idea, like a letter set in blocks. Strongest at 16 px, but it says less than the lock.

32 and 16 px

3 · Falling into place

The top block just above its slot, a moment before the fit. Best as the start frame of a short animation in the site header, where the block drops and locks.

32 and 16 px
