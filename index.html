Precision polymer engineering

Polymers without the average.

We build polymers one molecule at a time, then use that precision to control structure: at surfaces, on nanoparticles, and in imaging agents.

Cain Department of Chemical Engineering
Louisiana State University

Approach

A conventional polymer sample is an average of many different molecules. Any property you measure belongs to the distribution, not to a structure.

We remove the averaging. With molecularly uniform building blocks, architecture becomes a variable we set rather than one we infer.

Dispersity is not a detail. Identical chemistry, two side-chain distributions. The disperse brushes pack without order. The discrete ones align. Coarse-grained molecular dynamics at the air–water interface.
